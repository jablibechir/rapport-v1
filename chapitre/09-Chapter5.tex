\section*{Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Introduction}

Sprint 3 introduces the intelligence layer of the Sopra Recruit platform. This sprint focuses on automated CV analysis and candidate scoring to support HR decision-making and improve recruitment efficiency. The implemented features aim to provide clear, explainable, and actionable insights while preserving the existing recruitment workflow delivered in previous sprints.

\section{Sprint Objective}
% ============================================================

The objective of Sprint 3 is to enhance the recruitment platform with an AI-assisted evaluation workflow that automatically analyzes CVs and produces structured candidate scores. By the end of this sprint, HR officers should be able to view ranked candidates with clear scoring indicators, compare applicants more efficiently, and make faster and more consistent pre-selection decisions.

\hline
Use Case & CV Analysis and Scoring \\
\hline
Initiating Actor & HR Officer (Administrator) \\
\hline
Goal & Automatically analyze submitted CVs and compute a matching score against a target job offer to support pre-selection. \\
\hline
Pre-condition & A candidate application exists with a valid CV file; a target job offer exists with defined requirements and weighted criteria. \\
\hline
Post-condition & The system stores extracted CV information, the global matching score, and the detailed score breakdown; results are available in the HR interface. \\
\hline
Scenario Description &
The HR officer opens the applications list and triggers candidate evaluation. The system parses CV content, extracts structured attributes, compares them with offer requirements, computes weighted scores, and displays ranked results with explanation details. \\
\hline
Main Scenario (Success) &
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, label=\arabic*., topsep=0pt, itemsep=0pt]
	\item The HR officer selects a candidate application linked to a job offer.
	\item The system retrieves the CV file and offer requirements.
	\item The system parses the CV and extracts relevant attributes (skills, experience, education, certifications, and languages).
	\item The system normalizes extracted attributes into a structured profile.
	\item The scoring engine computes partial scores using configured weights.
	\item The system computes the final matching score and confidence indicators.
	\item The system stores analysis results and updates candidate ranking.
	\item The HR officer views the final score and detailed breakdown.
\end{enumerate} \\
\hline
Alternative Scenarios &
\textbf{A1 --- CV unreadable or corrupted:} The system marks the analysis as failed and requests a new CV upload. \newline
\textbf{A2 --- Missing criteria weights:} The system applies default weights and logs a configuration warning. \newline
\textbf{A3 --- Incomplete CV data:} The system calculates a partial score and displays missing information indicators. \\
